# Carom

Two paddles face off across a dark court. A bright ball serves into the opening rally, and every return asks you to choose an angle. Play the computer in Solo, or share a keyboard for a local Versus duel.

A moving paddle puts spin on the ball. Strike while sliding upward or downward and watch the trail bend across the court. A clean center hit buys time; a shot near the end of your paddle opens up a sharper route.

The amber obstacles make the middle of the field a bank-shot puzzle. Bounce around them, use them to send a return back across the court, or force your opponent to follow a changing line. The rally accelerates with every paddle hit.

At match point, one good angle can turn the score around. Reach eleven with a two-point lead to win; at a tie, the duel keeps going. Take a breath, find your angle, and play again.
